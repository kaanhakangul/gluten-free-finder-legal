Subscriptions

Manage or cancel Premium in iPhone Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions. To restore a purchase on a new device, go to Profile > Settings > Restore purchases in the app.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile > Settings > Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and your content.
