Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data the Gluten Free Finder app processes, why, and your rights. The data controller is Kaan Gül. You can reach us at lastyungmoney@gmail.com.

Data we collect

  • Account details: your email address, or what Apple or Google shares when you sign in with them. If you use the app without an account, your device gets an anonymous ID.
  • What you post: reviews, ratings, photos, place suggestions and content reports.
  • What you save: favorites and lists.
  • Location: used only with your permission, while the app is open, to show places near you. It is not stored with your account.
  • Product scans: the label photo you take is sent for analysis and not stored.

How we use it

  • To run the app: your account, saved items and posts.
  • To publish community content and prevent abuse.
  • For security and fixing problems.

We don't use your data for advertising, don't sell it and don't track you across other apps.

Service providers

  • Supabase (database, sign-in and file storage; servers in Germany, EU).
  • Anthropic (analysis of product label photos; a photo is processed only for that analysis).
  • Apple (Sign in with Apple and maps).
  • Google (Sign in with Google).
  • RevenueCat (managing in-app purchases and subscriptions; Apple takes the payment, and your card details never reach us or RevenueCat).

Some place information comes from open data such as OpenStreetMap and Overture Maps. Some providers may process data outside your country; such transfers rely on the safeguards required by law.

Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account exists. Settings > Delete Account permanently deletes your account and everything linked to it (reviews, photos, favorites, lists, suggestions).

Your rights

Under the GDPR, Turkish KVKK and similar laws you can access, correct, delete and export your data and object to its processing. Write to lastyungmoney@gmail.com; you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy and will announce important changes in the app. Last updated: 2026-11-05.
